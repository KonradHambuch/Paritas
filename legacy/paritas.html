Questions we always get

The honest answers, up front.

Are you tax advisors?
No — deliberately. We are transfer pricing economists and financial due diligence specialists: the analysis, the benchmarks, the documentation preparation and the numbers are ours. Where a matter requires licensed tax advice or formal sign-off, independent licensed partner tax advisors work inside the same engagement. You get one project, correctly staffed.
Who actually prepares — and who signs?
We prepare everything as economic and financial analysis on our standardized templates. Filings and matters reserved for licensed professionals in your country are executed by our partner advisors or your own accountant, working from our prepared pack — which is also why their hours, and your total cost, stay small.
How fast is fast?
TP Starter: days. Local File with benchmark: 2–4 weeks from complete intake. Red-Flag DD: 5–7 days. Series A financial DD: 2–3 weeks. Rush timelines exist and are priced in the calculator — the honest answer is that speed comes from your data quality, which is exactly what the free toolkit fixes in advance.
What do you need from us?
The intake questionnaire, a general-ledger export, statutory accounts, and whatever intercompany agreements exist. If you track flows in our free Excel register, the threshold analysis is already done when you arrive — and you get 10% off documentation for it.
We already have an accountant. Do they stay?
Yes, and they'll like us: we don't do bookkeeping, payroll or returns. We hand your accountant a finished documentation pack, invoice instructions and a compliance calendar — we make their TP problem disappear rather than competing for their work.
Why are you cheaper than mid-tier firms — what's the catch?
No catch, different economics: the expensive parts of TP and DD are data structuring and comparable searches, which we built once — standardized intake, template library, reusable CEE benchmark sets — instead of rebuilding them per client at hourly rates. You pay for judgment and review, not for reinventing the spreadsheet. Where you want a big-firm name on top, the partner-review tier adds exactly that.
Are the calculators legally definitive?
No. They are deliberately a decision-support layer, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific advice. Poland and Hungary have clear published thresholds, while Romania changed its transfer-pricing documentation framework in 2026; when the rule depends on taxpayer category, transaction type or a recent amendment, the calculator flags it for manual verification.
What happens to the data I enter in the calculators?
Nothing — it runs in your browser and is not stored or sent anywhere until you explicitly book a call or request the report. The outputs are informational estimates based on published thresholds, not tax advice.

The software

Five products on one spine.

The intake that produces your quote is the intake that produces your file. That single fact is why this costs less than a mid-tier firm and takes less time.

01 · Live on this site

The pricing engine

A firm quote in three minutes, with every factor shown. It tells you which rung you are on and why — and if a Local File is not obligatory for you, it says so out loud and quotes the cheaper rung. Verified across the range: €4,800 for a clean seed deal to €196,000 for a complex Series B.

02 · Delivery

The Local File generator

Group narrative, entity description, functional analysis, transaction mapping, method selection and jurisdiction formatting — generated from the intake. Sixty analyst hours become eighteen. The benchmark and the senior review stay human, because that is where the audit risk lives.

03 · The part that wins audits

Assisted benchmarking & the rejection log

Authorities rarely challenge which comparables you accepted. They challenge why you rejected the others. We automate the screening cascade and the rejection log, then a human makes the accept/reject call on the shortlist. Six hours becomes ten minutes of judgement.

04 · Recurring

Invoicing engine + threshold monitor

Compliant intercompany invoicing against your agreed policy, with the correct markup per flow. The monitor watches your cumulative transaction volume against the documentation threshold in each country — and tells you before you breach it, not after.

05 · Pre-raise

Data-room readiness score

Your data room mapped against the actual VC diligence checklist — corporate, cap table, IP assignment, contracts, employment, tax, financials. A completeness percentage and a prioritised fix list, before a fund sees it.

06 · For funds

Portfolio monitor

Portfolio companies upload monthly financials; the tool flags drift and structural change across the whole book. Combined with the free portfolio health-check, it shows which holdings have crossed a documentation threshold — before their auditor does.

How the loop closes: every engagement ends with a software offer, and every tool is instrumented to detect the moment paid work is needed. The threshold monitor firing is a qualified lead for a Local File. That is the design, not a coincidence.
